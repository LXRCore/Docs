The kit,
exact values.

Every colour, face and component from genatsvale.shop, extracted at the source and rewritten to survive the CEF build RedM ships. The band behind this headline is the wash — the two-radial gradient that makes flat black read as a lit room. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

01

The wash

Two offset radial gradients of the accent over void. It is the single most recognisable surface in the system, and the reason a panel reads as atmospheric rather than as a dead rectangle. On the site it sits behind the WebGL stage; in NUI it goes on a full-screen layer behind content — never on the panel itself. 

/* .lxr-wash — the percentages are the original values; the
   site's color-mix() calls are resolved to exact rgba here
   because CEF may not parse color-mix(). */
background-color: #050506;
background-image:
  radial-gradient(58% 48% at 50% 42%, rgba(141, 18, 38, 0.34) 0%, transparent 68%),
  radial-gradient(42% 38% at 78% 68%, rgba(194, 28, 55, 0.18) 0%, transparent 70%);
Body copy needs a scrim over it. The wash is atmosphere, not a background for text. Layer .lxr-scrim-grad between the wash and the content — that is what the darkening you see under this page's headline is doing.
02

Colour

Six inks and one accent. No pure #000 and no pure #fff anywhere: pure black flattens depth over a rendered game frame, pure white glares at night.

Ink

Accent — one hue, two steps

One accent. This is the hard rule. No gold, no bronze, no brown, no second hue. If two things compete and both want red, change one's weight or size — not its colour.

State — semantics only, never decoration

Contrast, measured against void

ColourHexRatioVerdict
bone#f4f2ee18.2:1AA + AAA, any size
ash#9b9aa07.3:1AA + AAA, any size
blood-lit#c21c373.4:1Large text only — ≥24px, or ≥19px bold
smoke#58575e2.9:1Fails AA at every size
blood#8d12262.2:1Fails — a fill colour, never text

Two consequences, and they are not optional. Smoke is for decoration and disabled states — the moment it carries meaning, use ash. Blood-lit works as a heading, an icon or a border, not as body copy; for a wine-coloured warning, put the wine on the border and the text in bone. Bone on blood-lit — the button hover state — is 5.3:1 and safe.

Inter · body · everything readable

Body copy sits in ash, not bone. Full-strength white on near-black at paragraph length is fatiguing; ash still clears AAA at 7.3:1 while sitting back far enough that headings keep their hierarchy.

04

The index row

★ Signature pattern

If you take one thing from this kit, take this. It is the site's nav overlay, and it is what makes an unfamiliar panel read as yours. Reach for it before you reach for a grid of cards or a table — it replaces almost every list a RedM script needs.

The anatomy

PartRule
.idx-nAlways mono, always accent, always zero-padded — 01, never 1
.idx-nameAlways the serif at scale. Never the sans.
border-topHairline on the top of each row, so a list reads open-ended rather than boxed in
.idx-metaRight-aligned by margin-left: auto, never a fixed column

What it replaces

MenusStores, stables, crafting, job boards, dialogue trees
InventoriesSaddlebags, satchel, wagon, stash, player trade
RostersPlayers online, gang members, employees, wanted list
RecordsTransactions, warrants, licences, deliveries, logs
SettingsAny enumerable list of toggles or bindings

Modifiers: --sub two-line · --compact dense · is-active selected · is-locked unavailable. Children: -index -name -body -sub -ka -meta -price -badge -lead -group.

Build the list from this row before you reach for anything else. A grid of cards is the generic answer and it costs you the one pattern that identifies the brand. If a screen genuinely cannot be a list, that is the moment to design something — not the default.

06

Over a game frame

The only honest way to judge a translucent surface. Both panels below carry the same text over a lit scene — watch what happens to the glass one.

Opaque panel
Legible over anything. Use this for every surface holding text.
Glass panel
Fine over dark ground, mud over a lit wall. Small floating controls only.
Panels are opaque on purpose. A translucent panel over a moving game frame becomes unreadable the instant the player walks past a lit wall. backdrop-filter also costs whole frames on a mid-range GPU — a full-screen glass panel loses FPS in a way players notice and blame your script for.

07

The brand mark

The LXRCore wolf is canonical for every lxr-* resource. It ships as a PNG at a fixed path — html/img/lxrcore-logo.png — because buyers and escrow tooling expect that exact filename. No SVG ships in a script.

Gold edge · README default
Wine edge · this kit
Hairline · neutral
No tile · dark ground only

Why the tile exists — measured, not assumed

The mark is white fill with a thin black outline. Sampled at 256px, 63% of its opaque pixels are light and only 25% are dark — so on a pale ground almost two thirds of the drawing drops out and what survives is a faint outline. It stays technically legible; it stops reading as a solid mark.

Cream · washed outCharcoal · correctVoid · correctWhite · washed out
Put a dark plate under it anywhere the chrome is light. Not because the mark vanishes — it does not — but because it loses the weight that makes it a logo.

On a real banner

Token and markup

/* :root */
--img-lxrcore-logo: url("img/lxrcore-logo.png");
--lxrcore-tile: #17171b;
--lxrcore-edge: #b8862f;  /* gold — override to #c21c37 for wine */
<div class="adm-store-banner__avatar-wrap adm-store-banner__avatar-wrap--lxrcore">
  <img class="adm-store-banner__avatar adm-store-banner__avatar--lxrcore"
       id="brand-logo"
       src="img/lxrcore-logo.png" alt="LXRCore" />
</div>
files { 'html/img/*.png' }   -- or CEF 404s it silently

Config.LXR.Brand.NuiLogo = 'img/lxrcore-logo.png'
-- merged into NUI locale as nui_logo; app.js updateChrome() sets
-- #brand-logo from nuiT('nui_logo', 'img/lxrcore-logo.png')

HUDs carry the mark as a background rather than an <img>, so it cannot be selected or dragged: set .lxr-session-hud .__brand and .lxr-guide-hud .__brand to --img-lxrcore-logo, contain, no repeat. .lxr-brand-watermark is the same mark at 6% for sitting behind text.

Gold or wine — that is a real decision, not an oversight. Your brand README specifies a gold tile edge for LXRCore. This kit is derived from genatsvale, which is wine-only by rule. They are different brands, so both are shown above. Override --lxrcore-edge; never edit the class.

<body class="lxr">
  <div class="lxr-br">HUD — never eats a click</div>
  <div class="lxr-center lxr-panel lxr-hit">Menu — clickable</div>
</body>

.lxr sets pointer-events: none on everything, because NUI is a full-screen overlay and without it your HUD swallows every click meant for the game. Opt interactive panels back in with .lxr-hit.

ESC must always close, and always call SetNuiFocus(false, false). A player stuck in a focused NUI with no way out has to restart their game.

Re-skinning another lxr-* resource

Override the eight root tokens. Nothing else.

/* theme.css — loaded AFTER lxr-ui.css */
:root {
  --lxr-blood: #1c4f8d;
  --lxr-blood-lit: #2f7fd4;
}

Do not override component classes. The point of a shared kit is that lxr-inventory and lxr-jobs cannot drift apart.
